CloudMounter Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

What the app accesses

When you connect a Google account, CloudMounter requests access to your Google Drive (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) and reads your account email address to label the drive in the menu. This access is used only to list, read, write, rename and delete files in your Drive at your request, so that your Drive appears as a disk on your Mac.

Where your data goes

All processing happens locally on your Mac. File contents and metadata are transferred only between your Mac and the cloud providers you connected. CloudMounter has no servers and does not collect, store, sell or share any of your data with the developer or third parties.

Sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac. Cached copies of files are stored on your Mac in the app's cache folder and can be removed at any time.

Google API Services User Data Policy

CloudMounter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove the account in CloudMounter and revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
